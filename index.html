MY EXPERIENCE / NOT JUST A LINE ON A MAP

There’s transit.
Getting there is another story.

Living between Burbank and Hollywood, I find it hard to reach places from either side. The beach, Downtown LA, or places farther away can take hours by public transportation. To me, the challenge is connecting all the pieces: getting to a stop, waiting, changing services, and still having time to enjoy where I’m going.

About this map

These are the subway services I selected, not a complete map of every train I have ridden or every NYC line. Station service labels are from the MTA daytime-service dataset; this is not a live trip planner. My comments about California describe my own experience living between Burbank and Hollywood. The LA map shows representative Metro rail, Metro bus, and BurbankBus route patterns, not live service or calculated travel times. NYC shows the twelve services I selected; the two views are not identical network samples.

Routes: MTA Subway Service Lines · Stations: MTA Subway Stations · LA layers: LA Metro GTFS / BurbankBus GTFS · Network snapshots retrieved November 23–24, 2026
